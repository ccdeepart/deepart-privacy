Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

In short: C.C’s DeepArt Studio does not collect, upload or sell any personal data and uses no advertising or tracking. Your photos are processed only on your phone.

Photos

The photos you choose to edit are opened and processed only on your phone, inside the app. The app can access only the photos you pick. Photos are never uploaded.

Camera

The overlay camera uses your phone’s camera so you can line up and shoot a new frame over an earlier photo. The picture is only shown on screen; the frame you shoot is edited on your phone and never sent anywhere.

Saving

When you save an edited photo, the app adds it to Photos on your phone. The saved file keeps the original photo’s capture information (EXIF, including date and location if the photo has them). Access to Photos is for adding only.

Stored on your phone

Styles you save and your language choice are kept only in the app’s storage on this device. Deleting the app deletes them too.

Internet

The app works offline. It uses no account, sign-in, advertising, analytics or crash reporting service.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published on this page with a new date.
