Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

In short: DeepArtCam does not collect, send to a server or sell any of your personal data, and it uses no advertising or tracking tools. Your photos, videos and sound stay on your phone.

Camera and microphone

The camera shows the live view and records your photos and videos. The microphone records sound with your videos and drives the sound meter. Everything is processed on your phone. If you do not allow the microphone, video records without sound.

Photos

The app asks only for permission to add to your Photos library. It saves your recordings there and cannot read your existing photos.

Image analysis

Colour correction, the image doctor, the instructor coach and subject tracking analyse the live image on your phone. Images are never uploaded anywhere.

Motion and pressure

Motion data is used to detect how the phone is held. In test versions, the phone’s pressure sensor is written to a file on your phone. That file stays on your phone unless you choose to share it.

What is stored on your phone

  • Whether you have seen the guide
  • A video that is being recorded, until it is saved to Photos
  • Test pressure logs, in test versions

If you delete the app, this information is deleted too.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether your subscription is active. A subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You can manage or cancel it in your Apple account settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Terms of use: Apple Standard End User License Agreement.

Internet connection

The camera works offline. The app connects to Apple only for subscriptions. It uses no account, sign-in, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting service of its own.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from any age group.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with its new date.
