Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

In short: C.C’s DeepArtEffect does not collect, send to a server or sell any of your personal data, and it uses no advertising or tracking tools. Your photos stay on your phone.

Photos

You choose a photo with the system photo picker. The app receives only the photo you pick and cannot browse the rest of your library. To save your result, the app asks only for permission to add to your Photos library; it cannot read your existing photos with this permission.

Camera

The camera is used only in Overlay camera mode, to take the second photo. If you turn on “Keep original”, the new photo is also saved to your Photos library.

Image analysis

Finding the subject, the effects and Overlay are processed on your phone. Photos are never uploaded anywhere.

What is stored on your phone

The app keeps no personal data. Your edits exist only while you work on a photo; the saved result goes to your Photos library. If you delete the app, nothing remains.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether your subscription is active. A subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You can manage or cancel it in your Apple account settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Terms of use: Apple Standard End User License Agreement.

Internet connection

Editing works offline. The app connects to Apple only for subscriptions. It uses no account, sign-in, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting service of its own.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from any age group.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with its new date.
