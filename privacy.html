C.C’sDeepArt Settings

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

In short: C.C’s DeepArt Settings does not collect, send to a server or sell any of your personal data, and it uses no advertising or tracking tools. Your photos and notes stay on your phone.

Photos

The photos you choose on the Analysis tab are read only on your phone, inside the app. The app calculates each frame’s shooting data (EXIF data such as aperture, shutter speed, ISO and date) and its brightness distribution. Photos are never uploaded anywhere, and the data that was read is discarded when the app closes. The app only accesses the photos you choose.

Dive log

The UDDF or Subsurface XML file you add is read only on your phone; its depth and temperature data are matched with your photos. The file is never sent anywhere.

What is stored on your phone

The following is kept only in the app’s storage on this device:

  • The camera, lens, strobe and shooting mode (photo/video) you chose, the entries in your gear bag, and your choice of units
  • Your log notes
  • Your ticks on the checklist and the items you added yourself
  • Whether you have seen the intro tour, and your language choice

If you delete the app, this information is deleted too.

Sharing and saving

When you save the settings card as an image or export the dive report as CSV, the file is created on your phone and your phone’s share menu opens. You decide where the file goes.

When you export a backup on the Log tab, your log, gear bag and your own checklist items are written to one file, which is saved only where you choose. When you restore from a backup, the file you pick is read only on your phone.

Internet connection

The app works offline. The app itself uses no account, sign-in, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting service.

The phone app carries its fonts and photo data reader inside the app and does not connect to the internet. In the web version, when you are online, fonts may load from Google Fonts and the photo data reader (only when you pick photos on the Analysis tab) from jsDelivr; these requests share only your browser's standard information (such as your IP address) with those services. Your photos, notes and settings are never sent in either version.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from any age group.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with its new date.
